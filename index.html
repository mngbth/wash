Keep Your Motorcycle Reliable and Safe

Regular Bike Wash

Bring your motorcycle for a wash every 2-4 weeks to remove salt, sand, dust, and dirt that can cause rust and wear. Regular cleaning helps us identify leaks, loose bolts, worn tyres, and other problems before they become expensive repairs. Motorcycles that are not washed regularly may suffer from corrosion, faded plastics, seized switches, and reduced resale value.

MVR 100/-

Engine Oil Change

Engine oil should be changed every three months or 2,000-3,000 km, whichever comes first. Fresh oil lubricates engine components, reduces wear, and helps keep the engine cool. Delaying oil changes can result in increased engine wear, overheating, poor fuel economy, loss of power, and costly engine repairs. A simple oil change can significantly extend engine life.

MVR 100/-

Wash and Polish

We recommend polishing every 1-3 months to protect your motorcycle from strong sunlight, salty air, and harsh weather. Polishing creates a protective layer that helps preserve paint, plastics, and metal surfaces. Without regular protection, surfaces can become dull, faded, oxidized, and more difficult to restore.

MVR 150/-

Gear Oil Change

Gear oil should be refilled or changed at regular intervals of 6-12 months to protect gears and bearings from wear. Fresh gear oil ensures smooth operation and helps prevent costly transmission problems. Ignoring gear oil changes may cause excessive wear, noisy operation, reduced performance, and expensive repairs.

MVR 50/-

Plastic & Switch Protection

Protecting plastics, rubber parts, switches, and exposed metal helps prevent fading, cracking, corrosion, and premature ageing. Regular treatment keeps your motorcycle looking newer, functioning properly, and better protected from salt and UV damage. Preventive care costs far less than replacing damaged parts.

Free

Tyre Pressure & Brake Fluid Service

Tyre pressure should be checked at least once a month for safer handling, better fuel economy, and longer tyre life. Brake fluid should be replaced every 2 years to maintain reliable braking performance. Neglecting these simple services can lead to poor braking, uneven tyre wear, reduced stability, and increased accident risk.

Free

Coolant Service

Coolant should be inspected every 6 months and completely replaced every 2 years. Coolant prevents overheating, lubricates the water pump, and protects the cooling system from corrosion. Old or low coolant can cause overheating, radiator damage, water pump failure, head gasket problems, and severe engine damage. Regular coolant service helps ensure reliable performance and avoids expensive repairs.

MVR 200/-
